# HIERA-VEP v1.0.0

Initial public research release of the locked context-gated Ridge model (alpha 1000), with a reproducible CPU batch scorer, saved preprocessing specification, exact additive contribution decomposition, summary scientific evidence, selected manuscript figures, and validation tests.

External zero-shot transfer is weak and heterogeneous. Assay adaptation is a secondary supervised analysis and is not zero-shot generalization. Predictions are compressed, large-effect variants remain a major failure mode, motif FDR support is limited, and significant exact GTEx overlap is extremely sparse. Concordance and model contributions do not establish causality.

Research-only use; not a clinical pathogenicity classifier. External datasets retain their original terms. Citation author metadata remain a clearly labeled placeholder pending authoritative final manuscript metadata.
